#4471 is more than one cause

owner Arkadiusz Nieckarz open reviewed 2026-11-03 · 0 days ago

#4471 merged every date report into one known issue. The cause is not the subject line. It follows the tenant: whether that locale writes the day first, and which booking form they run.

Two causes are in this ticket.

parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out flipped. That is the cause #4471 was filed as.

hint. On booking-v3 the form prints MM/DD/YYYY, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them. That is a different bug, and it has no ticket of its own.

Reports the desk read as a one-day move stay in the cause their tenant files them under. The table footnotes those.
